Getting started

  1. Add up to four players in the Players section, or tap a saved group to start with that roster. The scorecards stay locked until there are enough players for the selected game type: two for Individual or four for Team.
  2. Open Settings and choose a Game Type. You can change it before the first score is entered. After scoring begins, the choice stays locked until you start a new game.
  3. In Individual mode, every player has their own score in the leaderboard. In Team mode, Players 01 and 03 form one team, and Players 02 and 04 form the other. Individual player entries are added to their team's total.
  4. Players take turns using the active scorecard. Tap one of the score pads (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, or 8) to enter a score. The score flies to the player's leaderboard row in Individual mode, or their team's row in Team mode. Use Undo or Redo to reverse scorecard actions.
  5. When the round is ready to finish, tap End Round. Select everyone who has the lowest domino, then tap Confirm Round. Each selected player receives a 1-point bonus, which is included in the appropriate individual or team total. The next round then begins.
  6. The leaderboard and final standings sort by total. Team mode shows two rows and combines each pair's names with “&”. Use Graph to view individual totals or the two combined team totals over time. Score history keeps each player's entries visible.
  7. The game ends when an individual or team reaches the Points to Win target. The default is 120 points; change it in Settings. Use New Game to keep the current roster or clear it and add new players.
