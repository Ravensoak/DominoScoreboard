Getting started

  1. Add up to four players in the Players section, or tap a saved group to start with that roster. The scorecards stay locked until there are enough players for the selected game type: two for Individual or four for Team.
  2. Open Settings and choose a Game Type. You can change it before the first score is entered. After scoring begins, the choice stays locked until you start a new game.
  3. In Individual mode, every player has their own score in the leaderboard. In Team mode, Players 01 and 03 form one team, and Players 02 and 04 form the other. Individual player entries are added to their team's total.
  4. Players take turns using the active scorecard. Each player starts a round with 10 dominoes in a 2-player game, or 7 in a 3- or 4-player game. Tap a score pad (0, Pass, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, or 8) to enter a score. Pass scores zero but does not use a domino. The title bar shows how many dominoes each player has left. Use Undo or Redo to reverse or restore scorecard actions.
  5. In Individual mode, the round ends automatically when a player uses their last domino. They receive a 1-point First Out bonus, and a pop-up announces the round end. In Team mode, a player who runs out is skipped for the rest of the round. When their partner also runs out, the round ends automatically and the partner who ran out first gets the 1-point First Out bonus. Press Continue on the pop-up to move on.
  6. You can also finish a round early with End Round. Select everyone who has the lowest domino, then tap Confirm Round. Each selected player receives a 1-point bonus, which is included in the appropriate individual or team total.
  7. The leaderboard and final standings sort by total. Team mode shows two rows and combines each pair's names with “&”. Use Graph to view individual totals or the two combined team totals over time. Score history keeps each player's entries visible.
  8. The game ends when an individual or team reaches the Points to Win target. The default is 120 points; change it in Settings. Use New Game to keep the current roster or clear it and add new players.

Buttons and controls

  • ⚙ Settings: set the Points to Win target, choose Individual or Team mode, switch Light/Dark appearance, open this guide, refresh the app, or close the menu. Game Type locks after the first score. Refresh reloads the app; saved scores stay on this device, while unsaved settings or player names may be lost.
  • New Game: clear the scores and start again with the current players, or clear the roster and add new players. Cancel leaves the game as it is.
  • Score pads (0, Pass, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8): enter that player's score for the round. Pass records zero without using a domino. Only the active player can score. You can also select a numbered pad with its number key.
  • End Round: finish score entry early and choose all players who have the lowest domino. Confirm Round saves the round and awards each selected player 1 point; Cancel returns to score entry. First Out rounds end automatically when the required player or team runs out of dominoes.
  • Undo / Redo: reverse or restore recent scorecard actions. They are available when there is an action to undo or redo. Press Backspace to undo when you are not typing in a field.
  • Players – Add: add a name (up to four players). Tap a player's name in Individual mode to rename them; tap ✕ to remove them and their scores. Saved group names start a new game with that roster; ✕ beside a group deletes the saved group.
  • Graph: open the score graph. Back to scorer returns to the scoreboard. On the game complete screen, View score graph opens the graph and Return to scoreboard closes the results.
