Getting started

  1. Add players in the Players section. Tap a saved group to start a game with that roster again.
  2. Enter each round: tap each player's card values. Repeated values are allowed. Tap a tally chip to remove it, or use Undo and Clear.
  3. If a player went out first, mark First to Declare. The scorer applies Skyjo's double-score rule when they did not finish alone on the lowest score.
  4. Each player should review their tally and tap Commit Scores. If anyone changes their cards afterward, they need to commit again.
  5. Once every player has committed, End Round and Update Totals appears. Tap it to add the round to standings and history. You can edit a past score by tapping its number.
  6. The game normally ends when any player's total reaches 100. To keep playing when a player lands exactly on 100, turn on 100 Exact - Halve Score in Settings. After that round's scores and any closer penalty are applied, that player's total is halved and the game continues. A small ½ in the score history marks the round. The game ends as usual if a player's total passes 100.
